Frequently asked questions

What students usually ask.

A short guide to getting organized in college and studying with Nido.

How do I get organized to study in college?

Set up your degree by year and add the subjects you're taking. Each subject keeps its units, notes, files, assignments and your professors' details. The weekly calendar brings your classes and assignments together, today's tasks tell you what to do today and, every time you open Nido, Home suggests where to start.

Is Nido free?

Yes, Nido is a free study app for Windows and the web. The Free plan includes 1 degree, 1 course list, up to 5 active subjects, 200 flashcards and 100 MB for files. Passed or archived subjects don't count toward the limit.

How do I make a study plan for a midterm or a final exam?

In the subject, add the exam date and the topics it covers. Nido spreads them across the days you have left, saves the last day for a general review and tells you what to study today. If you fall behind, it redistributes what's left, so you never have to reorganize anything. It works the same for a midterm or a final: only the date changes.

What are flashcards and how do they help me study?

They're question-and-answer cards you create in each subject. Nido uses spaced repetition: after each card you pick Again, Hard, Good or Easy, and the card comes back right when you're about to forget it. You can add a routine like “Review flashcards for 10 minutes” every day.

Does Nido have AI to help you study or summarize PDFs?

Not yet. AI flashcards and summaries from your notes are in the works, with no release date yet. Today Nido helps you organize your material (your PDFs live inside each subject), build a plan for your exam and review with flashcards.

Does it have a study session timer, like Pomodoro?

Yes. Start a 10 to 45 minute session and the timer follows you around the app. Nido adds up your study hours for the week and your streak of days in a row.

Does it work for any degree?

Yes: university, college or standalone courses. You set up your degrees by year and each subject has its units, files, assignments and exams.

Can I use it on a Mac?

Yes, in your browser and with the same account. The downloadable app is for Windows; on Mac, Nido works fully on the web.

Does it sync between my computers?

Yes. Sign in to the Windows app or on the web and you'll see the same everywhere: subjects, notes, flashcards, your streak and your study hours.

Does it work without internet?

Offline you see what was last synced. Checking off a task, reviewing flashcards and study sessions are saved and uploaded automatically when you're back online. Creating or editing anything else needs a connection, and Nido lets you know.

Can I take my data with me?

Anytime: export your notes in Markdown, all your data in JSON and your flashcards in CSV from Settings.

Is Nido available in English?

This page is in English, but inside, the app is in Spanish for now. English and Portuguese are on the way.
